LOOK algorithm

The car keeps travelling in its current direction, stopping at every floor that has a lit car button or a hall call in that direction. When no requests remain ahead, it reverses (or goes idle if nothing is pending). Unlike SCAN it never travels to the end of the shaft unnecessarily, and unlike first-come-first-served it never zig-zags. Riders only board a car that is announced to go their way, and a full car passes hall calls by.
